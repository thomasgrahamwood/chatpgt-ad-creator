ChatGPT Ad Planner

ChatGPT ads have no keyword list. You describe the situation you want the ad to appear in, and the model matches it against the whole conversation. Every topic here produces a context hint, a question ladder across the buying journey, and five ad variants with their own angle and destination, in the language of the conversations you want to match.

Journey phase

Changes the question we buy, the context hint and the ranking. Most vendor questions already favour us, so earlier phases rank differently.

Buyer

Every question is written in the voice of this buyer, about a problem they actually have. A regulator and a network planner never ask the same thing.

Language

ChatGPT ads target by language. Write the hint in the language of the conversation you want to match, not only the ad copy.

Region

Country targeting is the only geographic lever in Ads Manager. This sets the country list in each brief and drops topics that do not travel.

Budget per campaign

Used only to estimate what each ad returns. Run as many as you like; the platform floor is 15 EUR a day per campaign.

Ranked topics

The five variants

Each topic carries five ads with structurally different angles rather than tonal rewrites: the pain the buyer stated, a proof point, a contrast against the approach they already use, a technical capability, and a reference or case. Each points at its own destination so you can see which creative pulls which page. Destinations marked "to create" do not exist yet and are the landing pages worth building first.

How to read the ranking

The order comes from four judgements: how well the topic fits what we actually sell, how strongly that conversation happens at the selected journey phase, how crowded it is with competitor messaging, and whether we already have a page to send the click to. There is no impression volume behind it, because ChatGPT ads report no search terms. Treat the order as an argument, not a measurement.

How the estimates are built

Cost per click is derived from how contested the topic is, from about 2 EUR on uncontested regulatory questions to about 3.20 EUR on crowded commercial ones. Conversion rate is set by journey phase, since an awareness click converts far worse than a vendor-shortlist click, and is adjusted by whether we have a landing page built for the topic. These are planning assumptions to be replaced with real numbers after the first month.

Why the decision phase ranks lower than it looks

A question like which vendor deploys a national cell broadcast system fastest already describes us closely enough that we tend to surface without paying. Topics where that is true are marked, and the ranking pushes them down at the decision phase. The budget is better spent where the buyer has not yet worked out that a public warning system is the answer to their problem.

Evidence and localisation

Proof points come from our published material: 50+ emergency system deployments, 500 million people protected, the Kuwait system live in 10 days, flood warning for 5.7 million at-risk properties in England, 40+ location techniques, CAMARA location, authentication and fraud prevention APIs, and Gartner recognition as a location intelligence player. Questions, hints and ad copy are localised. Planning notes stay in English.

Excluded and restricted

Financial services, health, gambling and cryptocurrency are excluded categories on the platform. Law enforcement creative sits close enough to the line that rejection is likely, so that topic carries a flag rather than a recommendation.
